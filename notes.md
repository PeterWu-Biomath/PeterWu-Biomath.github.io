Assorted notes on mathematical physics, integrable systems, and representation
theory. Each entry is a PDF compiled from the LaTeX sources in [`tex/`](https://github.com/PeterWu-Biomath/PeterWu-Biomath.github.io/tree/main/tex).

1. **[The Polyakov–Wiegmann Identity](/assets/01-polyakov-wiegmann-identity.pdf)** —
   A proof of the multiplicative property of the WZW action via Stokes' theorem.

2. **[A Concrete Example of the Zakharov–Shabat Construction](/assets/02-zakharov-shabat-construction.pdf)** —
   A worked example showing that the construction yields an integrable system.

3. **[Vertex Algebras and Algebraic Curves — Notes](/assets/03-vertex-algebras-and-algebraic-curves.pdf)** —
   Notes on Chapter 1 of Frenkel–Ben-Zvi: formal series and the definition of a vertex algebra.

4. **[A Linear Algebra Problem](/assets/04-a-linear-algebra-problem.pdf)** —
   A conjecture on $(X+Y)^{2p+2q+1}v=0$ arising from Drinfeld–Sokolov hierarchies.

5. **[Minimal Surfaces and the Polyakov Action](/assets/05-minimal-surface-polyakov-action.pdf)** —
   Polyakov action, conformal gauge, Weierstrass–Enneper parametrisation, and Bernstein's theorem.

6. **[Boson–Fermion Correspondence and the Jacobi Identity](/assets/06-boson-fermion-correspondence.pdf)** —
   Bosonic and fermionic Fock spaces and the boson–fermion correspondence.

7. **[Completeness of the Bethe Ansatz](/assets/07-completeness-of-bethe-ansatz.pdf)** —
   Counting Bethe roots via the BKK theorem, multiplicities, and roots at infinity.

8. **[A Guide to the Yellow Book](/assets/08-guide-to-the-yellow-book.pdf)** —
   Notes on Di Francesco–Mathieu–Sénéchal's *Conformal Field Theory*.
