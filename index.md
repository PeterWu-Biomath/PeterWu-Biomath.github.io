## About Me

I am an **independent researcher and programmer** with a background in mathematics,
physics, bioinformatics, and computational biology. My current interests include
combinatorics, phylogenetics, and exploring how large language models (LLMs) can
support mathematical research.

My previous work spans bioinformatics algorithm development and pathology image
analysis, most recently at **3DMed Diagnostics** in Shanghai.

## Current Research

I am exploring mathematical problems with the help of LLMs, experimenting with
ways to develop ideas, examine arguments, and connect mathematical reasoning
with computation.

- **[Hadwiger](https://github.com/PeterWu-Biomath/Hadwiger)** — An ongoing project
  exploring a mathematical problem with LLM assistance.
- **Combinatorics and phylogenetics** — My recent work studies forbidden traces
  of hypergraphs and the maximum size of compatible split systems.

## Software Projects

- **[Valis_X](https://github.com/PeterWu-Biomath/Valis_X)** — A simplified,
  in-memory refactoring of [VALIS](https://github.com/MathOnco/valis) for pairwise
  rigid and non-rigid registration of whole-slide pathology images.
- **[BWA-Capture](https://github.com/PeterWu-Biomath/bwa)** — An extension of BWA
  for targeted sequencing alignment, using a capture sub-index and
  perfect-alignment caching to accelerate reads from panel and exome regions.
